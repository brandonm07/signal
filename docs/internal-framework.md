# Signal Advisory — Internal Framework

Internal-use only. How we think about engagements. Not website copy.

---

## The Signal Advisory Framework

Four steps. Same order, every engagement, whether a ten-person business or
a multi-site mid-market client. Most agents skip the first one.

### See

Most businesses do not know what they are paying, to whom, or for what.
Step one is a single-page inventory: every monthly technology invoice,
every contract term, every renewal date. Hidden risk surfaces fast.

### Simplify

Mid-market companies typically pay four to six providers for services
that should sit with one or two. Consolidate without losing redundancy.
Fewer bills, fewer points of contact, less time spent managing vendors
who should be managing themselves.

### Secure

Every contract has gaps. Service credits no one claims. Exit rights no
one negotiated. Redundancy that exists on paper but not in practice.
Close the gaps before they cost a day of operations.

### Scale

Buy for the next location, the next acquisition, the next year of growth.
Not for what the carrier wants to renew. Structure contracts and
infrastructure so that scale does not require starting over.

## The Discovery Framework

Every first conversation answers these five questions. In order.
The answers shape the engagement. The roadmap comes out the other side.

### 1. Pain

What is frustrating today? Where is the spend out of control? Which
provider relationship is consuming time it should not?

### 2. Impact

What happens when it breaks? Where is the business actually exposed when
a circuit drops, a contract auto-renews, or a vendor consolidates without
notice?

### 3. Cost

What is overspend, downtime, and vendor friction costing right now? In
dollars, in hours, in revenue at risk.

### 4. Future

What does the next year of growth require? More locations, more
bandwidth, more security, more scale — each has a different contract
structure behind it.

### 5. Roadmap

The simplest path forward, in priority order, with the savings tied to
each step. No theory, no boilerplate. The exact moves, in the exact
sequence.

## How to use this document

- **Discovery calls.** Walk through the five questions in order. Take
  notes under each. The roadmap drafts itself by the end of the call.
- **Proposals.** Frame the executive summary around See / Simplify /
  Secure / Scale so the client recognizes the structure on the call.
- **Onboarding new advisors.** This is what we believe and how we work.
- **Board updates.** Tie quarterly progress to which step the engagement
  is in.
